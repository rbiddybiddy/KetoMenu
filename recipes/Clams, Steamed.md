## Clams, Steamed

- couple dozen clams
- 1 cup water
- melted butter

Wash clams thoroughly, removing any blemishes. Discard any that don't close when handled (they're dead) or any that have an odor (they're REALLY dead).

Add the clams and water to a pan or pot, cover and cook for about 10 minutes until clams open. Meanwhile you can melt the butter.
