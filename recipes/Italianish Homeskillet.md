## Italianish Homeskillet

This was partly improvised and partly from AI, so it’s hardly worth writing down, but what the hey.

- 12-16 oz Italian sausage
- half an onion, chopped
- 5 cloves garlic, minced or pressed
- 2 stalks celery, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 fresh tomatoes, chopped
- 14.5 oz can crushed tomatoes
- ½ tbsp salt
- 1 tsp pepper
- ½ tbsp basil
- ½ tbsp oregano
- olive oil for the pan
- rice: pre-cooked or uncooked (or omit for keto-ish)
- grated Parmesan for garnish
- fresh herbs for garnish

Cut up & brown sausage in skillet with olive oil over medium heat.

Add carrots and celery, a bit of olive oil if needed, and cook 5 min.

Add garlic, onions, salt, pepper, basil, oregano, and a bit more olive oil. Cook for a few min.

Add fresh & chopped tomatoes. If adding uncooked rice, add it now along with enough water to cook it.

Simmer until rice is done or carrots are tender.

If adding pre-cooked rice, add it now and heat through.

Serve topped with grated Parmesan, fresh herbs, and/or a drizzle of olive oil.
