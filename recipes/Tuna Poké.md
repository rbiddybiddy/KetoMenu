## Tuna Poké

- 2 lbs fresh raw albacore tuna cut into bite-size pieces(*)

Marinade:
- 1 cup soy sauce
- ½ cup rice vinegar
- ½ cup water
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
- ¼ cup sesame oil
- ½ cup sesame seeds
- 2 green onions, sliced thin
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 shallot bulb, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil

Mix you up some marinade and marinate the tuna in it for a while. Serve over rice or greens. Try a wakame salad maybe.

(*) Albacore is one of the most mercury-containing fish, so don't make a habit of it. On the plus side, it's naturally pretty low-risk for parasites and there's no FDA freezing or cooking requirement. You might nonetheless decide to go for frozen or certified sushi-grade anyway (something somebody stakes their reputation on).
